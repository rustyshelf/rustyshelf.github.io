Skala is a professional interface design tool built for speed, precision, and creative freedom. I've spent the last 4 years of my career working on it with the team at Bjango. Some might argue that this is the twilight of my career. The pinnacle. The most important part. Some people are weird like that, and also probably like vampires.

But it's finally out! Grab it now: https://bjango.com/mac/skala/.

Oh you're still here? Well as long as you downloaded Skala, please keep reading. You see the story of Skala goes back well over a decade, when Marc Edwards (a long time friend, Australian and fellow app developer) told me he was working on it. It was an ambitious project, but seemed to me to be within his wheelhouse. He was one of the most opinionated designers I knew, and he wanted to make an opinionated design tool. It made sense. I kept meeting him at various conferences and events, and he'd always say the same thing. To my question of "When is Skala out?" the answer was always "Soon! It's looking so good!". He did this for so long that I made a joke website about it (in November of 2013!). The website posed one simple question: Has Bjango released Skala Yet? Hard coded in the HTML was a simple, giant answer. Let me see if I can use my l33t html skills to re-create it right here.

NO

Oh yeah. I still got it. Well today, after 13 years (OMFG!) I updated it: https://www.hasbjangoreleasedskala.com/. Surreal is an understatement. What a crazy journey Skala has been on.

If you're reading this, please share the news! The world has changed, and it's hard to get the word out, but I know y'all are amazing and can help. Stay awesome.
